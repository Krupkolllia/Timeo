Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Introduction

Timeo ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Timeo application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our service and the choices you have associated with that data.

Data We Collect

We collect the following types of data:

  • Account Information: Email address, authentication details when you sign in
  • Shift Data: Work shifts, hours, dates, rates, and notes you enter
  • Device Data: Information about your device, browser, and app usage
  • Server Data: Timestamps, sync metadata, and error logs

How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide and maintain the Timeo service
  • Authenticate your account and sync your data across devices
  • Improve our service and fix bugs
  • Comply with legal obligations

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL database hosted on secure servers). We use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. Your data may be shared with:

  • Supabase (our cloud provider) for storage and sync
  • Cloudflare (our hosting provider)
  • Google (only when you choose to sign in with Google)
  • Law enforcement if required by law

Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data from the app. Deleted data is marked as soft-deleted and may remain in backups for a limited time before permanent removal.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data as a JSON file

Third-Party Services

Timeo uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Database and authentication
  • Google: OAuth authentication (optional)
  • Cloudflare: Hosting and CDN

Children's Privacy

Timeo is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
